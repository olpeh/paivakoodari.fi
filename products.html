Tarjouslaskuri

tarjous.online

A quoting tool for Finnish entrepreneurs. Make a quote in minutes: price and VAT are calculated automatically, and the finished quote downloads as PDF or Word with your company details and logo.

Customers, projects and schedules stay in one place. It works in the browser, also on a phone, so quotes can be made on site. Interface in Finnish, data stored in the EU.
